Intensive PT: Start when TSB is 3 mg/dL (51 µmol/L) above PT threshold, or if TSB rising >0.5 mg/dL/hr (8.5 µmol/L/hr).
ET indications: TSB ≥ ET threshold · Signs of moderate or advanced ABE · TSB above ET threshold (use whichever is higher).
Neurotoxicity Risk Factors: Isoimmune haemolytic disease · G6PD deficiency · Other haemolytic conditions · Sepsis · Significant clinical instability past 24h · Albumin <30 g/L.
NICE CG98 preterm (<35 wks): Values follow the official reference table (Phototherapy 40 µmol/L / Exchange 80 µmol/L at birth for every gestation, rising to a plateau at 72 hours of Exchange = gestational age (weeks) × 10 µmol/L, Phototherapy = Exchange − 100 µmol/L). Hours between 0–72h are linear interpolation, matching how the table's own 24h/48h columns are derived and rounded. Perform ET regardless of TSB if there are clinical signs of acute bilirubin encephalopathy.
