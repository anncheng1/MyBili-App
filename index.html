Intensive PT: Start when TSB is 3 mg/dL (51 µmol/L) above PT threshold, or if TSB rising >0.5 mg/dL/hr (8.5 µmol/L/hr).
ET indications: TSB ≥ ET threshold · Signs of moderate or advanced ABE · TSB above ET threshold (use whichever is higher).
Neurotoxicity Risk Factors: Isoimmune haemolytic disease · G6PD deficiency · Other haemolytic conditions · Sepsis · Significant clinical instability past 24h · Albumin <30 g/L.
NICE CG98 preterm: PT plateau after 72h = (GA × 10) − 100 µmol/L. Perform ET regardless of TSB if clinical signs of acute bilirubin encephalopathy. Use fibreoptic phototherapy first-line for preterm infants.
