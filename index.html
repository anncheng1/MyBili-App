NICE CG98 preterm (<35 wks): Values follow the official reference table (Phototherapy 40 µmol/L / Exchange 80 µmol/L at birth for every gestation, rising to a plateau at 72 hours of Exchange = gestational age (weeks) × 10 µmol/L, Phototherapy = Exchange − 100 µmol/L). Double-volume = 2 × blood volume estimate × weight, replacing ≈85% of circulating blood and removing ≈25% of total body bilirubin (extravascular bilirubin re-equilibrates into the vessels, producing the expected post-exchange rebound). Aliquot per Malaysian Paediatric Protocols, 5th edition: 5–6 mL/kg (capped at 5 mL/kg if ill or preterm), max 20 mL/cycle, not more than 5–8% of circulating blood volume per cycle; rate 3–4 min/cycle (≈1 min out, 1–2 min pause, 1 min in). Typical double-volume exchange: 90–120 minutes over 30–35 cycles. All fields editable — reduce aliquot/lengthen cycle time further for a less stable or very low birth weight infant.

Complications of Exchange Transfusion
Catheter-related
Infection · Haemorrhage · Necrotising enterocolitis · Air embolism · Vascular events · Portal/splenic vein thrombosis (late)
Haemodynamic
Overload / cardiac failure · Hypovolaemic shock · Arrhythmia (catheter tip near sinus node in right atrium)
Electrolyte / Metabolic
Hyperkalaemia · Hypocalcaemia · Hypoglycaemia or hyperglycaemia
Partial Exchange Transfusion
For polycythaemia (hyperviscosity) or severe anaemia without hypovolaemia — a smaller, targeted exchange rather than a full double-volume exchange.
—
Volume to exchange (mL)
Volume exchanged (mL) = Blood volume × (Initial PCV − Desired PCV) / Initial PCV, assuming whole blood volume ≈ 80 mL/kg. Desired PCV is a clinical decision (commonly ~55%, but confirm against current unit/consultant guidance) — this field is intentionally left blank by default.
